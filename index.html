Method

How to search, in order

  1. Set daily email alerts on these boards

    NSW Health jobs rarely appear on Seek, so the government boards matter most.

    I Work for NSWAll NSW agencies, many LHD roles. Save a search to get email alerts. NSW Health CareersEvery LHD and the Ministry. Set job alerts by keyword. APSJobsFederal agencies. Filter the location to Sydney. SeekPharma, medtech, insurers, NFPs. Save each title search. LinkedIn JobsSame, plus recruiters. Turn on Open to Work for recruiters only. Lifeblood careersAlexandria roles. They don't use agencies. ANSTO careersLucas Heights. Filter by business and commercial. Careers at CouncilGeorges River, Bayside, Sutherland Shire and more. EthicalJobsPHNs, charities and health not-for-profits.

  2. Search by classification as well as title

    Searching Grade 11/12, Health Manager Level 4 or EL2 together with words like data, performance or insights finds roles at the right pay with unfamiliar titles. The Titles tab has one-tap searches.

  3. Read the work type first

    Apply where the ad says Permanent or Ongoing. Skip Temporary, Contract, Fixed term and Grant funded unless the ad says the role can convert. Government talent pools are fine: one application can lead to several offers over 12–18 months.

  4. Share ads into this app

    In Seek, LinkedIn or Chrome, tap Share and choose Next Role. The Check tab scores the ad against your criteria, and you can save it to the tracker.

  5. Write for government selection

    NSW ads assess against the NSW Public Sector Capability Framework and usually ask 2–3 targeted questions. APS ads want a short pitch. Answer with numbers: territories designed, target attainment, incentive outcomes, pipeline and CRM adoption.

  6. Check paid parental leave timing

    NSW public sector staff get 14 weeks paid parental leave (2 more if partners share). The 2021 policy required 40 weeks of continuous service before the birth. APS agreements are moving to 18 weeks. If you're planning a baby, compare dates with your current employer's policy before moving.
